# Insight: Overthinking kills my voice

**Date:** 2026-09-01

**The insight (my own words):**
The more I think, the less I know what to say. Overthinking doesn't just delay me — it actively scrambles my voice. By the time I've over-analyzed, the thing I wanted to say is gone. So the fix isn't "think better" — it's capture before I think, not after.

**What triggered it:**
First raw capture in the second brain inbox. I went to write something down and realized the thinking-about-writing was already blocking the writing itself.

**Why it matters to me:**
This is the core of the overthinking blocker. It's not laziness or lack of ideas — it's a loop where more processing = less output. The 4-step workflow (capture raw, regroup later) is built for exactly this: intercept the thought before the loop starts.
