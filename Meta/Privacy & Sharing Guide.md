# Privacy & Sharing Guide

**For me only — not to be shared.** This is my map of what's in the vault, how sensitive each part is, and what to do if I ever want to show someone the project.

**Golden rule:** the *shape* of the system is shareable; the *contents* of my real life are not. No one needs to see my raw thoughts, my habits, my private history. They need to see the mechanism — and even then, curate it.

---

## Sensitivity map (what's where)

### High — never share raw, only with someone deeply trusted (or no one)
- **`Ariadne/Daily Thread.md`** — my current habits (wake-up, chores), my career struggles, deadlines, the "last chance" framing with my parents. Real evidence of my current state, including the hard parts.
- **`Inbox/Idea Inbox.md`** — raw unfiltered thoughts. Includes sensitive life details (sleep problems, a long-distance relationship from Paraguay, self-doubt, family context). This is private material.
- **`Ariadne/Presence.md`** — Ariadne's running read of me, including the trust dynamic and my self-understanding struggles.
- **`Meta/Passwords.md`** — actual credentials. Never, under any circumstances.

### Medium — shareable only with a professional (therapist/coach), and even then partially
- **`Inbox/Ariadne's Observations.md`** — patterns Ariadne noticed about me; insightful but probing.
- **`Insights/*`** — my realizations. Some are sharing-worthy; some are intimate. Selective.

### Low — the safe, proud, shareable parts
- **`Meta/Ariadne - Shareable Intro.md`** — written to be handed to anyone.
- **`Meta/How This Works.md`** — describes the system; has some personal framing but no private facts.
- **The overall structure** — the vault + workflow as a concept.

---

## What to do before showing anyone

1. **Show them the Shareable Intro first** — that's the version meant for human eyes. It explains the system, the fog, and why it matters, with zero private facts.
2. **If they want to see the actual vault:** open only the low/medium folders — Insights you personally pick, or the How-This-Works doc. Never open `Inbox/`, `Daily Thread`, `Presence`, or `Passwords`.
3. **If a therapist/coach wants depth:** you can go further with them — but still keep `Meta/Passwords.md` fully closed, and you decide which Inbox lines are relevant.

## If I ever feel pressured to share more than I'm comfortable with

Stop. It's my call what to show, and "no" is complete. Ariadne will back me up on this if I ask. Nothing here obligates me to expose private parts of my life to prove the project is real.
